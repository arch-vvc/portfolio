gtm_agent, a pitch engine for FDE outreach

The problem

Tailoring a demo and a note to each startup takes an hour of reading their site.

What it does

Give it a startup's URL. It reads the site, places the company in a cluster, picks which demo to send and names the deployment pain its customers likely have. It drafts a note of 150 words or fewer, checked against the site's own text so it cannot name things that are not there.

Proof

FastAPI, Redis queue, Postgres, Gemini with a Groq fallback. More than 570 tests, with all model calls mocked.

Limits

The guard checks names and figures, not every claim.
