Exception triage for accounts payable

The problem

AP teams match invoices, purchase orders and payments by hand, so wrong amounts, duplicates, missing POs and GST errors are found late.

What it does

Drop in three messy exports from Tally or Zoho Books. Get every mismatch with the rupees at risk, the exact rows and one plain English sentence.

Proof

It finds 16 of 16 planted exceptions across 8 types on a 40 invoice set shaped like real Tally and Zoho exports, with no false alarms on the look alike rows. 505 tests. It uses the standard library only and runs with no install.

Scope

CSV exports from Tally and Zoho Books, matched on purchase order, invoice and payment.
